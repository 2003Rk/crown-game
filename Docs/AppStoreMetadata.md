## Description
Catch the Jewels is a polished arcade game fit for royalty.

Rubies, sapphires, emeralds, amethysts and rare gold stars fall from the sky. Drag anywhere to slide your crown and catch them — but steer clear of bombs. You have three lives, and the jewels fall faster the longer you last.

• Simple touch-and-drag controls, with optional tilt steering
• Five jewels with distinct shapes and values
• Combo multipliers up to ×4 for catching jewels in a row
• Rare royal hearts that restore a lost life
• A new 60-second Daily Challenge every day
• 11 achievements and seven royal ranks to earn
• Satisfying effects, sounds and subtle haptics
• Accessibility options: Reduce Motion, larger crown, adjustable sensitivity, VoiceOver labels and Dynamic Type

Core gameplay works offline. No account or purchases are required. Points and achievements are just for fun and have no monetary value.
